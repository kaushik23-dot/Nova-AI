Good to know

Questions, answered straight.

How long does a website take?

Most builds go live in 2–4 weeks. Timelines depend on how quickly we lock content and feedback — I'll give you a clear schedule up front.

What's included in the build?

A fully custom, mobile-first multi-page site with copy help, on-page SEO, contact form, maps and booking links, launched on fast modern hosting. No page-count gimmicks — we scope what your business actually needs.

Who handles hosting?

I launch on fast, reliable hosting (Cloudflare Pages or Netlify). On the care plan I manage hosting and your domain for you. Prefer to self-host? That's fine too — you own everything.

Do I own my website?

Always. The design, the code and the content are yours from day one. No hostage situations, no proprietary lock-in.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Small edits are covered on the care plan. Bigger changes — new pages, seasonal campaigns — are quoted simply and fairly.

Is the care plan required?

No. It's optional and has no lock-in. Most clients keep it because a site that's maintained keeps working; you can add or drop it whenever.
