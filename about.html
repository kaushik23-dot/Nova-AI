About Nova

One person.
Two toolkits.

Nova is a one-person studio in Rolleston, Selwyn. I build websites for local businesses and make AI-powered short-form video for online brands — using modern tooling to deliver work that used to need a whole team.

Your portrait here

The short version

Studio quality, minus the studio.

I started Nova on a simple belief: the businesses that make a place special — the café on the corner, the trade you'd recommend, the small brand with a great product — deserve the same calibre of work the big players pay agencies a fortune for.

AI changed what one focused person can deliver. I pair that speed with real design judgement and honest communication, so you get output that looks expensive without the agency invoice or the six-week email chain.

Based in Rolleston, working with clients across Selwyn, Christchurch and — for video — anywhere online.

Why it works

Two kinds of client, two kinds of edge.

Local — websites

The local advantage

For businesses in Selwyn and Christchurch, I'm close, personal and invested. You get a real person who understands the area, meets when it matters, and treats your site like it's on the main street — because your customers are.

Online — video

The volume advantage

For DTC brands, the game is creative volume. AI tooling lets me produce a high output of test-ready video at a price that makes sense — so your ad account never runs dry of fresh hooks to try.
